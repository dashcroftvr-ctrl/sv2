Muddy

What can I do with Muddy? Friendly, flirty and boot-obsessed — riggers, Timberlands, Chelsea boots. Walk up and he'll say hello; hang around and he chats (out loud) and offers boot jokes — nod for yes, shake for no.
Look down at his boots for a second and he'll notice. Stare at him and he'll notice that too. Hearts may be involved.
Poke him with a fingertip or controller — chest, head or the boots. Poke too much and he stomps.
Animation menu floats to your left: press with your fingertip, or point and pull the trigger. He comments on every move — try the Love You Dance.
Thumbstick left/right = previous/next move · Grip = hide/show menu · Boots! = he shows them off.
On this screen: click him (or his boots) to poke, arrows = next/previous move, Y / N to answer a joke offer.
